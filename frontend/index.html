      How this works

      Checkpoints fire on a 2-minute interval if the text changed, on a large paste, after +200 characters, on a structural edit, when a source is added, or manually. Each checkpoint is hashed as SHA-256(previous hash | time | text). The server re-verifies the chain. This shows process, not authorship.

          Background agent

          The local connector runs on the student's laptop, where their Schoology session lives. It sends assignment titles and dates to the app — never cookies or passwords.

          Privacy

          No keystroke logging. No AI-detector score. Anonymous codes only. Receipts expire after 90 days. Parents see summaries, never raw drafts.

          Shows evidence of process, not proof of who wrote it. Students choose what to share.
